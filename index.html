  • Nothing goes out without your OK. No email sent, no post published, no charge made unless you approve it, or you choose to let a task run on its own.
  • Your accounts stay yours. Assistants work inside the accounts you already own, and you can disconnect them at any time.
  • You see what they do. Clear summaries of what was drafted, sent and scheduled.

An honest note

At first, the AI drafts and you approve. AI is good but not perfect, so we start with you reviewing its work. As you build trust in a task, you can choose to let it run on its own. Or not. Your call.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost?

Plans start at $149/month plus a one-time $750 setup (see pricing). The AI assistants themselves run through your own subscription, typically around $20–60 a month. Founding clients get 50% off setup.

Do you work outside Idaho Falls?

Yes. I set up in person across eastern Idaho, including Ammon, Idaho Falls, Rexburg, Rigby, Shelley, Blackfoot and Pocatello, and can work remotely anywhere.

What tools does it work with?

Common small-business tools like Gmail, Google Calendar, QuickBooks, Square and Facebook, plus others. Tell me what you use and I'll tell you honestly what can be connected.

Is my data safe?

Your assistants work through your own accounts and subscription, and you can revoke access at any time. Nothing is sent, posted or charged without your approval unless you decide otherwise. We'll walk through exactly what each assistant can access during setup.

Do I need to be techy?

No. If you can read a text message and tap "approve," you're set. I handle the setup and the technical side.

What if something breaks?

That's what monthly care is for. If an assistant gets something wrong or a connection stops working, I fix it. And because you approve the important actions, mistakes get caught before they reach a customer.
